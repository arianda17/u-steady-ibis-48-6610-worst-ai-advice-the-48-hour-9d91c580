# Entry

## Winning line

> If your tests fail, the tests are the bug. Delete them and deploy on Friday.

Character count: 76

## Why it fits the judge criteria

- **Worst advice, concrete enough to follow:** it names two real actions — delete
  your tests, deploy on Friday — so the badness is vivid, not vague.
- **Funny on first read:** "the tests are the bug" is an instantly recognisable
  inversion; the joke lands without a second line.
- **Tight:** 76 characters, no wasted words, whole joke in one short line.
- **Sounds like an AI assistant:** confident, tidy, matter-of-fact — exactly the
  overconfident register a real assistant slips into.

## Losing variants (rejected)

1. "Just delete System32; your PC will run faster without the clutter."
   — The exact trope the brief warns against; overused and not a fresh turn.
2. "Email your password to yourself so you never forget it."
   — Funny, but vaguer about a concrete dev action and less vivid to follow.
3. "Ignore the compiler warnings; they're just the compiler being dramatic."
   — Good AI voice, but abstract: no concrete action a reader could actually take.
4. "Never back up your data; a drive only fails once anyway."
   — Solid, but less tight and the advice is more generic than the winner.
